## Cross-paper pattern

The acquired steel papers separate a small analytical structural operator from a path-independent capacity mechanism and can close with finite algebraic roots. The acquired RC sources replace Airy/Galerkin membrane fields by strip/beam-column or FE fields and require section moment–curvature iteration, spatial compatibility and peak/search termination. This is a mechanics difference, not merely a software preference.
