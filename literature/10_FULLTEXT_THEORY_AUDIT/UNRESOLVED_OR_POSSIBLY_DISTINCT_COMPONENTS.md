3. An analytically justified finite control-location set for steel–concrete/UHPC walls, rather than an assumed FE/test location or a full spatial search.

5. A zero structural empirical-reduction formulation that still handles cracking, tension stiffening, compression softening, connector slip and local plate buckling.
6. A criterion that distinguishes when a direct finite-root limit state is physically adequate and when material-history/arc-length analysis is unavoidable.
