# NZ-SCCM history — explicit-capacity priority and material-underuse reset

**Date:** 2026-08-10 20:56 +08:00

## Trigger

After R01–R06 progressively reframed the problem from a difficult 2D current surface to scalar/material-compiler complexity, the user clarified that the project was again over-optimizing the material representation.

The key clarification was:

> A measured/material peak or local sharp feature does not have to be used to its full extent. It may be deliberately rounded, lowered or simplified as a data-processing decision. Any mathematical representation is acceptable — 4D stress-strain surface, global target function, invariant/spectral representation, special function, etc. — provided the final ultimate capacity is obtained from explicit formulas and explicit derivatives.

## Correction to project emphasis

Previous stages treated several items as progressively stronger intermediate objectives:

- unified current-map grammar;
- exact rank-4 spectral factorization;
- scalar compactification;
- D15/kernel family selection;
- global polynomial vs Möbius head-to-head.

These remain useful evidence and candidate tools, but they are no longer the project-defining objective.

The project-defining objective is now the **end-to-end explicit derivative chain to ultimate capacity**.

## Material interpretation

The material model is permitted to be a deliberately simplified analytic target rather than a pointwise replica of every source/oracle feature.

A local sharp corner/ridge can be treated similarly to an experimentally measured sharp peak that an engineering model deliberately does not exploit fully. The modification must be documented in mechanical terms:

- compression reduction;
- tension reduction;
- TT/TC reduction;
- enhancement;
- mixed effect;
- tangent smoothing.

The accepted simplification is judged by its transparency, physical plausibility, derivative compactness and final structural validation, not by minimum pointwise source error alone.
