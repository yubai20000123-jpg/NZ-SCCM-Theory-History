                     摘要




                    摘要

  钢箱混凝土结构在工程应用中展现出显著优点，包括高强度、高耐久性、优秀的抗
震性和抗爆性，以及施工快速方便等。其应用范围广泛，尤其适用于中高层建筑、工业
建筑和桥梁建设等领域。钢箱混凝土结构能够有效地满足工程对于强度、耐久性和可
塑性的要求，为现代建筑和桥梁工程提供了可靠的结构解决方案。
  钢壁板作为钢箱混凝土结构的重要组成部分，当其受到外部荷载作用时，可能会
出现屈曲失稳的现象，这不仅会降低其承载能力，还可能导致结构的整体破坏。因此，
深入研究钢壁板的屈曲失稳行为，对于确保钢箱混凝土结构的稳定性和安全性具有重
要意义。然而，由于钢壁板的一侧受混凝土挤压，所以在屈曲时面外位移只能向另一侧
发展，这提高了屈曲研究的复杂程度。现有的钢壁板屈曲理论研究主要集中于小挠度
理论，屈后行为的研究主要依靠试验和有限元，而有限元方法由于接触问题和非线性
问题收敛性极差，还没有一种考虑薄膜应力的大挠度解析理论来预测期屈后行为。针
对此问题，本文的研究内容和成果如下：
  （1）本文从钢壁板屈曲的面外变形条件和固支边界条件出发假定了挠曲形函数的
形式，利用 Karmen 大挠度方程得到应力函数的解析表达，最后通过求解伽辽金方程得
到了考虑单侧约束钢壁板几何初始缺陷的屈后强度解析解。该理论在工程应用和学术
研究中为钢壁板的屈后行为研究提供了理论依据。
  （2）同时，本文建立了一种考虑钢壁板初始缺陷的收敛良好的 abaqus 静力学模
型，结合解析理论以长宽比、宽厚比、初始缺陷值为基本变量参数化分析了钢壁板的行
为。分析了钢壁板的屈曲路径、位移和轴向应力的分布规律、屈曲应力和极限应力。同
时对比分析了理论与有限元结果的差异。结果表明：屈曲波在面内的分布主要受长宽
比 a/b 的影响。理论和有限元分析均显示，随着长宽比的增大，屈曲波的数量增加，且
二者在波的数量和位置上保持一致。较大的长宽比更易导致板件屈曲，屈曲半波的数
量取最接近 a/b 的正整数。轴向正应力在面内的分布同样受 a/b 比的影响。理论和有限
元在屈曲波波峰位置应力分布一致，但理论分布更为平滑，有限元则因接触作用在接
触位置附近产生一定程度的应力集中。
  而屈曲路径、屈曲应力和极限应力主要由宽厚比 b/t 决定。较小的 b/t 值导致更短
的屈后阶段和更高的应力值，最终接近钢材屈服强度。考虑材料塑性时，小宽厚比下极
限应力可能超过屈服强度，最大增幅达 14%。在 b/t=110 时，理论屈曲应力达到最大，
为 29.8%，而极限应力差异在大宽厚比时最大，为 28.6%。本文还分析了板的几何初始
缺陷对屈曲行为的影响，无缺陷板在屈曲荷载下开始产生面外位移，而初始缺陷板在

                                        ABSTRACT

     The steel box concrete structure exhibits remarkable advantages in engineering
applications, including high strength, excellent seismic and blast resistance, as well as rapid
and convenient construction. It finds extensive applications, particularly in mid- and high-rise
buildings, and bridge construction. The steel box concrete structure effectively meets the
requirements of strength, durability, and plasticity in engineering, providing a reliable
structural solution for modern buildings and bridge projects.
     The steel wall panel, vital in steel box concrete structures, risks buckling instability under
external loads, reducing load-bearing capacity and possibly causing structural failure. Hence,
studying its buckling instability is crucial for stability and safety. However, buckling's out-of-
plane displacement is complex due to concrete compression on one side. Existing theories
focus on small deflections, while post-buckling behavior relies on experiments and finite
element methods. This paper addresses the lack of analytical theory for large deflection post-
buckling behavior by considering membrane stress. Addressing this issue, the research content
and outcomes of this paper are as follows:
     (1) Starting from the out-of-plane deformation conditions and clamped boundary
conditions of steel wall panel buckling, this paper assumes the form of deflection shape
functions. Using Karman's large-deflection equations, an analytical expression for the stress
function is obtained. Finally, by solving the Galerkin equation, an analytical solution for the
post-buckling strength of steel wall panels considering geometric initial defects under
unilateral constraints is derived. This theory provides a theoretical basis for the study of post-
buckling behavior of steel wall panels and offers a more efficient computational method
compared to finite element and experimental methods in engineering applications and
academic research.
     (2) Meanwhile, this paper establishes a well-converged Abaqus static model considering
the initial defects of steel wall panels. Combining analytical theory, parametric analysis is
performed on the behavior of steel wall panels using length-to-width ratio, width-to-thickness
ratio, and initial defect value as basic variables. The buckling path, displacement, and axial
stress distribution, as well as buckling stress and ultimate stress, are analyzed. A comparative
analysis is also conducted between the theoretical and finite element results. The results show
that the distribution of buckling waves within the plane is primarily influenced by the length-

in the number and location of waves. A larger length-to-width ratio is more likely to cause
