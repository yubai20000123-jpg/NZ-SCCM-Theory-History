     The buckling path, buckling stress, and ultimate stress are primarily determined by the
width-to-thickness ratio b/t. A smaller b/t value leads to a shorter post-buckling stage and
higher stress values, ultimately approaching the yield strength of steel. When considering
material plasticity, the ultimate stress under a small width-to-thickness ratio may exceed the
yield strength, with a maximum increase of 14%. When b/t=110, the theoretical buckling stress
reaches a maximum of 29.8%, while the difference in ultimate stress is largest at a large width-
to-thickness ratio, at 28.6%. Additionally, initial defects reduce the buckling stress of steel box
concrete wall panels. Panels without defects undergo out-of-plane displacement under
buckling loads, while panels with initial defects begin to displace under smaller loads. As the
defect increases, the buckling path loses its inflection point, and the load-displacement curve
exhibits a uniform upward trend. The theoretical results are very close to the finite element
results under small width-to-thickness ratios and tend to be safer under large width-to-
thickness ratios. The buckling characteristics obtained in this analysis provide valuable
references for the study of post-buckling behavior.
     (3) Furthermore, based on the derived analytical theory of large-deflection buckling for
steel wall panels, this paper further derives a theoretical formula for effective width. By
combining experimental data from the literature, the theoretical formula is modified, and the
modified formula can better predict the width-to-thickness ratio limit.

method; effective width

1.1 研究背景及意义
  钢箱混凝土是一种结合了钢和混凝土优势新型结构形式，由钢箱内部填充混凝土
得到。相较于普通的预应力混凝土连续箱梁，这样的结构形式有效地降低了自重。在施
工方面，可以通过合理的结构设计并利用预制桥面板作为现浇桥面板的底模，选择泵
送技术实现混凝土浇筑，以提高桥面横坡施工精度和施工效率。因其高承载力、优越的
延性和抗震性能，以及防火性能优于纯钢结构等优点被广泛应用。钢箱混凝土通常用
于大型桥梁的构造，尤其是跨度较大，需要承受较高荷载甚至是处于恶劣环境下的桥
梁，其良好的耐久性和高强度得到充分的发挥。此外，在高层建筑、工业厂房和地下结
构中，由于钢板的存在其延性和抗震性能提高，钢箱混凝土能够作为稳定性良好的支
撑结构。




                    图 1-1 钢箱混凝土桥工程实例
        Fig. 1-1 Engineering example of steel box concrete bridge

  钢箱混凝土壁板在受载过程中可能产生局部屈曲。这种局部屈曲主要发生在试验
梁跨中区段，其屈曲范围与钢板厚度有关，钢板越薄，局部屈曲范围越大。局部屈曲通
常从跨中顶板开始向外鼓曲，随着荷载的增加，受压区腹板也可能产生局部向外屈曲。
屈曲行为可能会对结构的安全性和稳定性产生影响，因此在设计和施工过程中十分重
要。
