# Key equations — single-halfwave postbuckling membrane audit

Let

`X=pi*x/b`, `Y=pi*y/a`, `w0=A0 sinX sinY`, `wm=A sinX sinY`, `S=A^2+2A0*A`.

Nguyen Eq.(6.3) second-order mid-plane increments:

`eps_x^NL = S*pi^2/(8*b^2) [1+cos2X-cos2Y-cos2X cos2Y]`

`eps_y^NL = S*pi^2/(8*a^2) [1-cos2X+cos2Y-cos2X cos2Y]`

`gamma_xy^NL = S*pi^2/(4*a*b) sin2X sin2Y`  (engineering shear convention).

Incremental FvK geometric compatibility bracket:

`w_xx w_yy - w_xy^2` relative to the stress-free initial-imperfection reference gives

`-(S/2)*(pi/b)^2*(pi/a)^2 [cos2X+cos2Y]`.

Therefore the single `(1,1)` out-of-plane halfwave induces at least two independent pure second-harmonic membrane compatibility source directions `(2,0)` and `(0,2)`, in addition to mean/homogeneous boundary-load fields.
