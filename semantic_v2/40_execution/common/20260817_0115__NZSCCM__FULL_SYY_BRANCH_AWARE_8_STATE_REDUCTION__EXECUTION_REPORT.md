## 2. Key spectral observation

For any symmetric 2x2 strain matrix `E`, let

\[
\Delta_E=(\operatorname{tr}E)^2-4\det E,
\qquad
g=\sqrt{\Delta_E},
\]

\[
\lambda_\pm=\frac{\operatorname{tr}E\pm g}{2}.
\]

Both exact Foster spline thresholds `lambda1` and `lambda10` are thresholds of scalar matrix functions of the **same E**. Therefore every one-eigenvalue-active positive-part correction uses the same spectral projectors

\[
P_\pm
=\frac12\left[I\pm\frac{2E-\operatorname{tr}(E)I}{g}\right].
\]

The two knot families do not require two independent projector radicals.

Moreover,

\[
(\operatorname{tr}(E-\lambda I))^2
-4\det(E-\lambda I)
=\Delta_E,
\]

independent of `lambda`.

Therefore the knot-1 and knot-10 source corrections share one and the same gap radical `g`.
