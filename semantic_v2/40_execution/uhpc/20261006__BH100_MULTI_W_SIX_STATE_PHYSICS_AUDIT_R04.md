R03 was executed over w = 80, 82.2, 85, 87.323, 90, 95 mm instead of at one isolated deflection.
The UC141 continuous monotone material polynomials from R02 were retained.

## 2. Why it fails

R03 still made one hidden assumption inherited from the old Airy elimination: the absolute membrane compression state was reconstructed from w and the plate reaction itself.
That means a large measured/current out-of-plane deflection was allowed to determine the absolute UHPC principal strain state.
In the real axial-compression problem, total end shortening / mean axial membrane strain is an independent path coordinate. Out-of-plane geometry determines geometric shortening and curvature but cannot by itself determine the absolute compressive membrane strain.

The DIRECT peak frame supplied in the project has BH100 axial shortening 17.478 mm together with deflection increment 87.323 mm. Hence the peak state contains both an axial path coordinate and an out-of-plane coordinate. Eliminating the axial coordinate is physically inadmissible.

The historical Yun interface already contains the missing decomposition:
epsilon_bar_s = sigma_Y/E_s + C_epsilon*(2 A0 A + A^2).
The first term is material axial strain; the second is geometric shortening.
R03 retained essentially only the second mechanism when constructing the common plate operator.

## 3. Revised common principle

Keep exactly six INTERNAL state variables:
(D_c*, p_c, D_+*, p_+, D_-*, p_-).

But do not confuse internal state variables with external path coordinates.
At each state one external axial compression coordinate lambda (or Delta/L) and one current transverse geometry coordinate w are allowed inputs/path variables. They are not additional material parameters.

For each plate j, write:
epsilon_total_j = epsilon_material_j + epsilon_geometric_j(w,w0,p_j).

Plasticity/damage updates only D_j* and p_j.
The uniform/mean axial compression is never inferred from w alone.

## 4. One-scalar plastic imperfection must be obtained by work projection

Do not map pointwise plastic strain directly into curvature and then into p_j.
For a reduced one-parameter Yun geometry, p_j must be the scalar projection of the complete plastic generalized strain field onto the Yun permanent-geometry direction.

Let g_j(p) be the vector of membrane/curvature strain changes generated by changing the reference geometry from w0 to w0+p.
Let eps_p,j be the material plastic generalized strain field and C_r,j the current recoverable stiffness.
Define p_j by the stationarity condition:
Integral [g'_j(p_j)]^T C_r,j [g_j(p_j)-eps_p,j] dV = 0.

This is the energy-orthogonal one-coordinate condensation.
It automatically decides how much plastic membrane strain and plastic curvature can actually be represented by ONE Yun-style permanent imperfection.
Plastic components orthogonal to this direction are not falsely converted into global p_j.

## 5. Damage/yield roles

UHPC damage: only modifies current recoverable stiffness C_r and hence D_c*.
Steel ideal plasticity: no material damage; elastic unloading stiffness remains E_s. Local Yun post-buckling geometry plus yielded-zone state determines the effective structural D_s*; fy caps stress/resultant.
Plastic strain in both materials enters only through the work projection defining p_j.
