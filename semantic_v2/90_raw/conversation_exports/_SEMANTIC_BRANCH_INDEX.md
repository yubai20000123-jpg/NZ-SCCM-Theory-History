# Raw conversation-export semantic branch

Raw user-assistant conversation exports and original execution tool records have the highest project-history evidence priority when available.

Legacy locators are registered through `history/raw/`, `history/RAW_HISTORY_REGISTRY.md`, and `evidence/catalog/MASTER_SOURCE_INVENTORY.csv`.

Raw exports are immutable evidence. Summaries/recovery notes may locate them but may not overwrite or invent missing turns/tool outputs.
