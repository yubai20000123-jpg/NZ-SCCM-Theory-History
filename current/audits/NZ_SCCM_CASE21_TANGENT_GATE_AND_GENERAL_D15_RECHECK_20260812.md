## 1. Governing current-tangent identity

The canonical theory defines

\[
\mathbb C_t(X,Y,\zeta;D,q)=\frac{\partial\boldsymbol\sigma}{\partial\mathbf E}
\]

and for the basic Navier perturbation

\[
\varphi=\sin X\sin Y,
\]

\[
\mathbf b_\varphi=-z\begin{bmatrix}\varphi_{,xx}\\\varphi_{,yy}\\2\varphi_{,xy}\end{bmatrix}.
\]

The material, geometric and reinforcement tangent terms are

\[
K_{Z,c}^{mat}=\int_{\Omega_h}\mathbf b_\varphi^T\mathbf C_t^{eng}\mathbf b_\varphi\,dV,
\]

\[
K_{Z,c}^{geo}=\int_{\Omega_h}(\sigma_x\varphi_{,x}^2+2\tau_{xy}\varphi_{,x}\varphi_{,y}+\sigma_y\varphi_{,y}^2)\,dV,
\]

\[
K_{Z,s}^{mat}=\sum_{r,\alpha}t_{s,\alpha}^{(r)}\int_A E_{s,t}^{(r,\alpha)}[z_s^{(r)}\mathbf n_\alpha^T\boldsymbol\kappa_\varphi\mathbf n_\alpha]^2dA,
\]

\[
K_{Z,s}^{geo}=\sum_{r,\alpha}t_{s,\alpha}^{(r)}\int_A\sigma_{s,\alpha}^{(r)}(\mathbf n_\alpha\cdot\nabla\varphi)^2dA,
\]

\[
K_Z=K_{Z,c}^{mat}+K_{Z,c}^{geo}+K_{Z,s}^{mat}+K_{Z,s}^{geo}.
\]

All structural integrals are to be contracted by the same general D15 exact moments.

## 2. Important implementation scaling check

Because

\[
\mathbf X=\frac{\mathbf E_u}{\varepsilon_0},
\]

a perturbation derivative must obey

\[
\delta\mathbf X=\frac{\delta\mathbf E_u}{\varepsilon_0}.
\]

The factor `1/epsilon_0` is mandatory in the current-tangent directional derivative.

For Case21 at the unloaded state `(D,q)=(0,0)`, the exact isotropic square-halfwave regression is

\[
K_Z(0,0)=\frac{E_0t_p^3\pi^4}{12(1-\nu^2)b^2}.
\]

Substitution of

\[
E_0=20321\ \mathrm{MPa},\quad t_p=19.30\ \mathrm{mm},\quad \nu=0.18,\quad b=1220\ \mathrm{mm}
\]

gives

\[
\boxed{K_Z(0,0)=823.416805665\ \mathrm{N/mm}}.
\]

The coefficient-space directional differentiation reproduces the same value to numerical roundoff when the `1/epsilon_0` scaling is included. This is adopted as a mandatory zero-state tangent regression gate.
