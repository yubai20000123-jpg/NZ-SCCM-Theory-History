  钢-预制 UHPC 开孔板组合桥面板界面抗剪性能研究
                        中文摘要

      本文研究的钢-超高性能混凝土（UHPC）组合桥面板是一种新结构，其 UHPC
层以预制开孔板（预制板）为主，预制板间设现浇带（湿接缝）。现浇带上设栓钉作
为组合板的基本连接；预制板预留孔中设群钉、板底设水泥基粘结剂连接层，作为
预制板的强化连接。本文进行群钉连接（群钉连接件）、水泥基粘结剂连接（粘结剂
连接件）、栓钉与水泥基粘结剂组合连接（组合连接件）抗剪推出试验，开展精细化
有限元计算和理论分析，研究各界面抗剪性能包括承载力、滑移和刚度。主要工作
与成果如下：
      （1）以栓钉直径为参数，完成了 4 个群钉连接件推出试验。结果表明，间距均
为 50 mm 时，两种直径的栓钉均表现为根部剪断；栓钉直径为 13mm 时，UHPC 板
仅在焊缝挤压区域出现局部破损，但当栓钉直径为 16 mm 时，UHPC 板表面出现横
向贯穿裂缝，宽度超过 0.04mm。采用经试验结果验证的精细化有限元模型进行扩展
参数分析，结合文献资料与理论分析，提出考虑焊缝和群钉效应影响的抗剪承载力
计算方法、滑移模型和刚度计算取值方法。
      （2）以粘结剂种类、龄期、温度和粘结面积为参数，完成了 24 个粘结剂连接
件推出试验。结果表明，常温下高性能水泥基粘结剂（HPCA）抗剪强度约为普通
水泥基粘结剂的 2 倍，主要表现为粘结剂内聚破坏；HPCA 抗剪强度随龄期呈早期
发展快，28 d 基本稳定；抗剪强度随粘结面积的增大会有所降低，降幅在 3.1~8.4%。
高温下，HPCA 的抗剪强度会降低约 50%，但降低后的强度仍超过 1.0 MPa，主要表
现为与 UHPC 板脱粘。根据试验结果，建立了 HPCA 抗剪强度预测公式。
      （3）以栓钉直径、数量和布置方式为参数，完成了 12 个组合连接件推出试验。
结果表明，粘结剂在失效前，能与栓钉协同受力；失效后，所引起的刚度退化和荷
载突变对栓钉的损伤较小，栓钉的抗剪强度仍能完全发挥。粘结剂能显著提高组合
连接件的早期抗剪刚度，是栓钉试件抗剪刚度的 2.6 倍以上。试件在预制板与湿接
缝交界处、预制板与剪力槽交界处均出现了横向裂缝，缝宽超过 0.04 mm。
      （4）应用经试验结果验证的有限元模型的扩展参数分析表明，组合连接件的受
力性能受粘结剂与栓钉的抗剪承载力比值 N A /N s 的影响。 N A /N s  0.3 时，粘结剂失
效后，刚度退化但所受荷载并不下降，可不计其影响； 0.3  N A /Ns  0.9 时，粘结
剂失效后所受荷载小幅下降，第一峰值荷载值 Nu,1 小于第二峰值荷载 Nu,2 ；当
N A /Ns  0.9 时，粘结剂失效后所受荷载大幅下降，Nu,1 大于 Nu,2。根据试验和有限
元结果，建立了组合连接件的抗剪承载力计算方法和抗剪-滑移预测模型。

关键词：预制 UHPC 板；栓钉；粘结剂；抗剪性能；承载力；滑移；刚度；有限元

           Research on Interface Shear Behavior of
         Steel-Precast UHPC Composite Bridge Slab
                                     with Pocket
                                         Abstract

     The steel-Ultra high Performance Concrete (UHPC) composite bridge slab studied in
this paper is a new structure. Its main part is precast UHPC slab with shear pocket (precast
slab in short), which is connected by cast-in-place UHPC belt (wet joint). The headed studs
in the cast-in-place UHPC belt are used as the basic connection of the composite slab.
Precast UHPC slab is combined by group studs in shear pocket and cement-based adhesive
connection layer at its bottom, these connections are served as its strengthening connection.
In this paper, the shear push-out tests of interface between steel plate and UHPC deck
combined by group studs (group studs connector), cement-based adhesive (adhesive
connector), and studs and cement-based adhesive combined (combined connector) were
carried out. On this basis, the refined finite element calculation and theoretical analysis are
further researched to study the interface shear performance, including bearing capacity,
slip and stiffness. The main work and achievements are listed as follows:
     (1) Taking the diameter of stud as parameter, 4 push out tests of group studs
connector were carried out. The results show that the headed studs with two diameter both
appeared to be stud shank failure as the spacing was 50 mm. When the stud diameter was
13 mm, the UHPC slab was only partially damaged in the extrusion area of the weld.
However, some transverse through crack on the surface of the UHPC slab with the width
of more than 0.04mm as the stud diameter increase to 16 mm. In addition, the refined finite
element model verified by the test results were used for extended parameter analysis.
Combined with literatures and theoretical analysis, the calculation method of shear bearing
capacity considering the influence of weld and group effect was proposed, as well as the
slip model and stiffness calculation method.
     (2) Taking adhesive type, age, temperature and bonding area as parameters, 24 push
out tests of adhesive connector were carried out. The results show that the shear strength of
high-performance cement-based adhesive (HPCA) was twice that of normal cement-based
adhesive (NCA) at normal temperature, which mainly happens adhesive layer destruction.
The shear strength of HPCA developed rapidly in the early stage and was basically stable
at 28 days. And the shear strength of HPCA was decreases by 3.1-8.4% with the increase
of the bonding area. At high temperature, HPCA was mainly debonded from UHPC slab,
and its shear strength will be reduced by about 50% with minimum strength of 1.0 MPa.
Finally, the formula for calculating the shear strength of HPCA connector was established
basing test data.
        (3) Taking diameter, number and layout of the headed stud as parameters, 12 push out
tests of combined connector were carried out. The results show that the adhesive can work
together with the stud before debonding failure. After failure of adhesive, the stiffness
degradation and load mutation which caused less damage to the stud, and stud can make
full use of its strength. The use of adhesive can significantly improve the early shear
stiffness of combined connectors, which is more than 2.6 times that of stud specimens. In
addition, transverse cracks were appeareed with a width of more than 0.04 mm appeared in
the interfaces between precast slab and wet joint as well as precast slab and pocket.
        (4) The extended parameter analysis of the finite element model verified by the test
results showed that the mechanical performance of the combined connector was related to
the shear bearing capacity ratio of the adhesive and the studs ( N A /N s ).When N A /N s ＜0.3,
the adhesive failure will lead to the stiffness deterioration of the specimen, but the load
reduction was not obvious. Therefore, the influence of the adhesive failure can be ignored.
When 0.3≤ N A /Ns ＜0.9, the load reduction was slightly and the first peak load value (Nu,1)
was less than the second peak load (Nu,2) with adhesive failure. However. when
N A /Ns  0.9 , the load reduction was significantly and Nu,1 was greater than Nu,2 with
adhesive failure. Basing the test and finite element results, the calculation method of shear
capacity and shear slip prediction model of combined connector were established.

Keywords: Precast UHPC Slab, Headed stud, Cement-based
adhesive, Shear, Bearing capacity, Slip, Stiffness, Finite element

                     第一章 绪论


1.1 选题背景

   正交异性钢桥面板具有自重轻、承载能力大等特点，已广泛应用于大跨径钢桥
中，为减轻自重，它一般以沥青为桥面铺装层[1,2]。然而，正交异性钢桥面一直存在
钢桥面铺装层损坏和钢桥面板疲劳开裂这两大病害[3,4]。采用一层极薄的超高性能混
凝土（Ultra-high Performance Concrete，简称 UHPC）作为应力过渡层，形成钢-
UHPC 组合桥面板（图 1-1），是在保持自重轻的前提下克服上述两大病害的重要途
径。




                   图 1-1 钢-UHPC 组合桥面板

   UHPC 是一种超高强、高韧性、高弹模的新型水泥基复合材料[5]。通过剔除粗
骨料，采用优质细骨料，优化水泥、硅灰、细骨料和其他矿物掺合料的颗粒级配，
