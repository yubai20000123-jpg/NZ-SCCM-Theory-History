## Decision

`R09C_ENERGY_POTENTIAL_STRUCTURAL_TARGET_PRECHECK = STOP_NOT_EXECUTED`

Reason:

The proposed R09C implementation would identify a whole-structure potential `W(D,q)` from already evaluated structural response data. Under the current R07R/R08/R09A proof-of-concept machinery, those structural response data are generated by offline high-accuracy spatial quadrature over the complete halfwave. Although the final fitted `W(D,q)` and all of its derivatives would be explicit, the coefficient-identification chain would still contain spatial numerical integration.

Therefore this implementation does **not** satisfy the project's formal zero-spatial-numerical-integration doctrine.

## Important distinction

The **energy-potential principle itself is compatible** with the zero-numerical doctrine if and only if:

1. the material potential `Psi(epsilon1,epsilon2)` is an explicit analytic function (or finite analytic piecewise function) with explicit derivatives;
2. the whole-halfwave structural potential
   `W(D,q)=Integral_Omega Psi(epsilon(D,q,x,y,z)) dV + W_s(D,q)`
   is generated by D15 exact moments / finite analytic contraction / accepted small named closed-form kernels;
3. no spatial Gauss/Simpson/adaptive/offline numerical quadrature is used to determine `W`, its coefficients, or its derivatives;
4. `P`, `Rq`, and the tangent/limit condition are obtained by differentiating that same analytic `W`.

Under those conditions:

`P = (1/c_D) W_,D`,
`Rq = W_,q`,
`L = (1/c_D)(W_,DD W_,qq - W_,Dq^2)`

would satisfy the zero-numerical formal route.

However, that analytic generation has **not yet been demonstrated for the current material potential**. Therefore no structural-energy fitting/precheck is authorized now.

## Material fitting boundary

Material-level parameter identification from material test/source data is not, by itself, spatial numerical integration. It may be used to define an explicit analytic `Psi`, provided the formal structural operator thereafter uses only analytic contraction. Numerical optimization for material coefficients is a data-identification step, not a spatial quadrature operator.

But numerically integrating stress curves to create a hidden material-energy table and then interpolating it should not be promoted as the formal material operator when an analytic primitive can be constructed directly.

## Current action

- No new structural `W(D,q)` global fit.
- No new Case21 rerun under this energy-target route.
- R09A results remain diagnostic only.
- Energy-potential formulation is retained as a *candidate analytic architecture*, not as an accepted production implementation.

If that analytic derivation cannot be completed without spatial numerical quadrature, the energy-potential structural route must remain stopped.
