Philip A. Griffiths, *On the periods of certain rational integrals. II*, Annals of Mathematics, Vol. 90 (1969), pp. 496–541. DOI: `10.2307/1970747`.

Primary locations checked:

## 2. Köck & Tait 2018

Bernhard Köck and Joseph Tait, *On the de-Rham cohomology of hyperelliptic curves*, Research in Number Theory 4, Article 19 (2018). DOI: `10.1007/s40993-018-0111-4`; arXiv: `1709.03422`.

The paper gives an explicit basis framework for first de-Rham cohomology of hyperelliptic curves.

NZ-SCCM role: supporting mathematical context for using a finite de-Rham basis on the genus-2 hyperelliptic curve identified in R02.
