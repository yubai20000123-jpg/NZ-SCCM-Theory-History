## Effective evidence retained

The source derives equi-biaxial tensile properties by inverse analysis of ring-on-ring tests and independently derives uniaxial tensile properties from 4PBT for comparison.

For the reported UHPFRC:

- mean equi-biaxial elastic modulus: about `59,000 MPa`;
- mean equi-biaxial elastic-limit stress: `9.14 MPa`;
- mean equi-biaxial strain at end of hardening/localisation-related point: `1.27%`;
- mean equi-biaxial tensile strength: `14.08 MPa`.

The source comparison states that the equi-biaxial elastic-limit stress is about 18% lower than the corresponding uniaxial value (`11.16 MPa`), while equi-biaxial tensile strength is nearly equal to the uniaxial tensile strength (`13.99 MPa`). The equi-biaxial strain at the end of hardening is only about 28% of the reported uniaxial value.

This is important because it provides **strain-coordinate evidence in the TT quadrant**, not only a strength-envelope point.
